Clinical Case Atlas Bangladesh is a free, mobile-friendly study companion for doctors preparing for postgraduate medical examinations in Bangladesh.

Our Purpose

To bring cases, viva preparation, practical examinations, spotters, imaging and investigation interpretation together in one organised place, covering medical, surgical, diagnostic, basic science, public health and dental disciplines.

It is designed for candidates of FCPS, MD, MS, MCPS, Diploma, MPhil and other recognised postgraduate qualifications in Bangladesh.

How to Use the Site

  • 1. Find your specialty in the directory
  • 2. Open a learning module such as viva or spotters
  • 3. Bookmark items and revise them later
  • 4. Use the search bar on the home page to jump anywhere

Content & Verification Policy

Qualification, eligibility, syllabus and examination format are different for every specialty and institution. We do not claim that every qualification exists in every specialty.

Qualification details are added only after they are checked against official Bangladesh sources, such as the Bangladesh College of Physicians and Surgeons (BCPS), Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujib Medical University (BSMMU) and the universities that offer the courses. Until an entry is verified, it is marked as not yet added.

Privacy

No account, login or payment is needed. Your bookmarks and recently viewed items are saved only in your own browser using local storage. They are not sent to any server and we cannot see them.

Clearing your browser data, using private browsing, or changing device will remove or hide them.
